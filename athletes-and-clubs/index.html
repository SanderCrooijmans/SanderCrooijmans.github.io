Athletes and clubs

Planning a recovery space for a club

A shared chamber is a facilities decision as much as an equipment one. These are the questions we would settle first.

Scheduling and throughput

  • How many people will want sessions, and how long will each booking occupy the room, including set-up and cleaning between users?
  • Who is responsible for supervising sessions, and what does the manufacturer say about supervision and training?
  • Oxynova describes Series 9 as hosting up to two users. Ask whether that applies to your use and what the written limits are.

Hygiene and upkeep

  • Ask for the manufacturer's written cleaning instructions for the liner, mattress and windows, and for approved cleaning products.
  • Ask what inspection and replacement schedule applies to zippers, seams, hoses and filters under shared use.

Space and support

  • Plan floor space, a power supply for the compressor, and a quiet place for it. Oxynova states a sound level under 60 dBA.
  • Confirm commercial warranty terms, delivery, installation, staff training and who to call if a chamber is out of service.
Before you offer sessions. A club that offers sessions should get written guidance on user screening and liability from the manufacturer, its insurer and a qualified clinician.
