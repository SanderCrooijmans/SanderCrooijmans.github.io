Oxynova's overview page says its chambers operate at 1.4 ATA generally, while its Series 8 and 9 pages state 1.3 ATA, so confirm the working pressure for the exact model in writing. The same 0.562 m³ volume figure is printed for every model despite different sizes, so we leave it out. Series 8 and 9 weights and capacity statements come from separate product pages. Outer dimensions are not interior space.

Check the fine print. The label on a photographed Series 8 chamber reads “Not intended for use with enriched oxygen.” Do not assume any chamber is suitable for oxygen enrichment. Ask Oxynova to confirm in writing what is and is not permitted for the model you are considering.

Which questions separate the models?

Space

Series 9 is the longest and widest on the published figures. Measure your room, doorways and stairs, and plan space for the compressor and hose as well as the chamber.

Who will use it

Oxynova describes Series 9 as hosting up to two users and Series 8 as suited to professional settings. A stated capacity is not a recommendation for any person or condition.

Support and terms

All three carry the same stated warranty. Ask about delivery, installation, training and what happens if a part needs replacing.
