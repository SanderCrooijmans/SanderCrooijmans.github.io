Static view

Tap a numbered dot to see what it is. Drag to look around.

  • Easy-entry zipper openingA long opening along the top makes getting in and out straightforward. Oxynova describes a double or triple integrated zipper system. The opening shown is an artistic cutaway.
  • Open liner on a steel frameAn internal stainless steel frame holds the chamber’s shape, so the interior stays open around you. Oxynova says it assembles with quick connections and no tools.
  • Orthopedic mattressOxynova describes an orthopedic mattress with a phthalate-free cover: a supportive, comfortable surface to lie back and relax on.
  • Windows for light and a viewOxynova lists 5½ in windows, with counts that vary by model (this illustration shows three per side). Daylight comes in and you can see out, so the space does not feel closed in.
  • Air valve and pressure gaugeOxynova’s photographs show an air valve and a pressure gauge on the outside of the shell, so the chamber’s settings are easy to see.
  • Head end and hose connectionA fitting at the head end connects the compressor line. Oxynova lists a 9 ft (2.5 m) hose and an 800 W compressor with a stated sound level under 60 dBA, so the compressor can stand a short distance away.

Questions to settle before purchase

  • Is this model approved, cleared or CE/UKCA-marked for my intended use in my country? Ask for the answer in writing.
  • What are the interior dimensions, and how does a person get in and out?
  • What are the manufacturer's written safety instructions, including what must never be used inside?
  • What power, floor space and ventilation does the compressor need?
  • What are delivery time, installation help, training and replacement-part availability?
  • Is any current offer or financing available for my location, and what are the exact terms?
  • Is this suitable for me? Only a qualified clinician who knows your health history can answer that.
