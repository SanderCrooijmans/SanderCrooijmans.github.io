Businesses

Procurement checklist for recovery businesses

Buying a chamber for customers or patients carries regulatory, legal and safety obligations. This page lists what to verify before you commit. It is not legal or medical advice.

Check the regulatory documents for your use. Ask Oxynova for the documentation that applies to the exact model and your country, and get advice before you offer sessions to customers. Soft-shell mild-pressure chambers and clinical hyperbaric oxygen therapy are different, so do not assume that clinical findings or rules for one apply to the other.

Checklist

  1. Regulatory status. Ask Oxynova in writing for the regulatory status of the exact model in your country (in the United States any FDA listing or clearance number; in the EU or UK any CE or UKCA documentation), and the intended use stated on the label. Verify it with the public database or authority yourself.
  2. Intended-use wording. Oxynova's chamber labels state they are not intended for use with enriched oxygen. Ask what that means for any oxygen device or accessory you might plan to use, and do not combine equipment without the manufacturer's written approval.
  3. Who may use it. Ask what screening, contraindications and supervision the manufacturer requires, and get clinical and legal advice for your jurisdiction. Do not collect health histories through this website.
  4. Training and support. Ask what staff training is provided, who answers safety questions, and what response times apply to a faulty unit.
  5. Maintenance. Get written cleaning, inspection and replacement schedules for commercial use, and the cost of consumables and parts.
  6. Insurance and liability. Ask your insurer whether offering sessions on this equipment is covered, and under what conditions.
  7. Commercial terms. Confirm warranty scope for commercial use, delivery, installation and financing terms in writing.
  8. Marketing claims. Do not repeat health or outcome claims from manufacturer materials unless you can substantiate them for the exact product and use.

United States and Europe

Requirements depend on the country. In the United States, ask for the FDA status of the exact model. In the European Union and the United Kingdom, ask Oxynova in writing whether the exact model carries a CE or UKCA marking as a medical device or under another regime, for the declaration of conformity and for the intended use on the label, and check your national authority's rules on offering sessions to customers. Do not assume that a status in one country applies in another.
