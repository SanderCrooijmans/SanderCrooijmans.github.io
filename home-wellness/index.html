Planning a chamber at home

A home chamber is a piece of equipment that lives in a room. Most of the decisions that matter are practical, and they are easier to make before you order.

The room

  • Measure the chamber's outer length and diameter from the model pages, then add space to walk around it and to reach the compressor and hose.
  • Check the route in: doorways, stairs and turns. Oxynova lists chamber weights from 18 to 28 kg, but the compressor and cradle are separate.
  • Choose a flat, hard floor, away from heat sources and direct sun. Ask the manufacturer for its written placement guidance.

Power, noise and routine

  • Oxynova lists an 800 W single-phase compressor and a sound level under 60 dBA. Decide where it will sit, and whether that noise suits the room.
  • Think about cleaning the liner and mattress, storage of the hose, and where family members or pets will be during use.
  • Ask how the chamber is inflated, deflated and inspected, and what the manufacturer says must be checked before each use.

Discretion and ownership

Some buyers care about how a chamber looks in the home, who else can reach it, and how it is serviced. Ask Oxynova about delivery, installation help, training and replacement parts, and get the warranty in writing.

Before you use a chamber. If you have a medical condition or take medication, speak to a qualified clinician before using any pressurized chamber.
