Frequently asked

Is this the official Oxynova website?

No. This is an independent site run by an Oxynova affiliate. We link to oxynova.com, and we may earn a commission if you buy through those links.

What is a soft-shell chamber?

A soft-shell chamber is an inflatable fabric chamber supported by an internal frame and pressurized slightly above room air by a compressor. Oxynova describes its chambers as working at about 1.3 to 1.4 ATA, far below the pressures used in hospital hard-shell chambers.

Can you tell me what a chamber will do for my health?

No. This site does not make health or performance promises and cannot give medical advice. Soft-shell chambers and clinical hyperbaric oxygen therapy are different things. Talk to a qualified clinician about your situation and ask the manufacturer for its written guidance.

Are these chambers cleared by the FDA?

We could not confirm any FDA clearance or approval statement for Oxynova chambers on the manufacturer's website, so we do not say they are cleared or approved. Ask Oxynova in writing about regulatory status for the use you have in mind.

What does it cost?

We do not publish prices. The manufacturer's published figures do not state a currency, term or eligibility, and we cannot confirm any current offer. Ask Oxynova about current offers and financing for your location.

Which countries does this site cover?

The United States and Europe. Rules for these chambers differ by country: ask Oxynova in writing about the regulatory status of the exact model for your country (for example FDA information in the United States, or CE or UKCA documentation in the EU and UK) and about delivery to your address. Canada is not covered, because Health Canada states that no soft-shelled hyperbaric chamber is authorized for sale there.
