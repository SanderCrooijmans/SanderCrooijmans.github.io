Frequently asked

What is a soft-shell chamber?

A soft-shell chamber is an inflatable fabric chamber supported by an internal frame and pressurized slightly above room air by a compressor. Oxynova describes its chambers as working at about 1.3 to 1.4 ATA, far below the pressures used in hospital hard-shell chambers.

Should I speak to a clinician first?

Yes, if you have a medical condition, take medication, or have ear, sinus, lung or eye issues. A qualified clinician can advise on your situation, and Oxynova can give you its written guidance for the model you are considering.

What is the regulatory status of the chambers?

It depends on the exact model and your country. Ask Oxynova for the documentation that applies to you, for example FDA information in the United States or CE or UKCA documentation in the EU and UK, and check the public databases yourself.

What does it cost?

Pricing and financing depend on the model and your location. Ask Oxynova about current offers, eligibility and delivery to your address.

Which countries does this site cover?
